FAQ

Questions answered.

Who is this for specifically?

If you know the basics of SEO and want to offer it as a service to clients, you're in the right place. We help agencies, consultants, and freelancers build better systems, get better results for clients, and do it with less manual work.

Why should I listen to you?

Ryan Stewart has 15+ years building and selling SEO agencies. He currently operates a portfolio of marketing companies generating over $10M/year in revenue. Everything taught here is built from real experience — not theory.

Do I need SEO experience?

You'll get more from the program with basic SEO knowledge, but you don't need to be an expert. We'll make you one. The program is built for people who want to offer SEO as a service and build proper systems around it.

What's the difference between Grow and Scale?

Grow gives you everything you need to build and run a high-performing agency at your own pace. Scale adds direct 1:1 coaching, a custom growth track, accountability check-ins, and Ryan's Inner Circle for agency owners who want to move faster with expert support.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes — 30 days, no questions asked for Grow. Go through the first modules and if you don't believe this will change how you run your agency, email [email protected] for a full refund.

Can I speak to someone before joining?

Yes. Email [email protected] with any questions, or book a call on the Get Started page. If you're a past Blueprint member, email Elena and we'll find your record and set up a discounted rate.
