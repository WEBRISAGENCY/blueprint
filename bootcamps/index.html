Common Questions
FREQUENTLY ASKED
How long is each bootcamp?
Bootcamps run 2-4 weeks depending on the topic. Simpler topics like Niche Selection run for 2 weeks, while more complex builds like Service Productization run for 4 weeks. Each bootcamp has a fixed start and end date with clear milestones each week.
What if I can not attend the live sessions?
Every session is recorded and available within 24 hours. You can also submit your homework and questions asynchronously, and the instructors will review them during the next session. That said, members who attend live consistently get the best results because of the real-time feedback.
How much time do I need to commit each week?
Plan for about 3-5 hours per week. That includes attending the live session (about 90 minutes), completing homework assignments, and participating in the cohort channel. The homework is designed to build real systems you will actually use, so the time investment pays for itself immediately.
Are bootcamps included in my membership?
Yes. Bootcamps are included in your Blueprint Training membership at $199 per month. There are no extra fees, no upsells, and no premium tiers. You get access to every bootcamp, plus coaching, training, community, AI agents, and everything else in the program.
Can I join multiple bootcamps?
Yes, but we recommend focusing on one at a time for maximum implementation. New bootcamps launch monthly based on member demand, so there is always another one coming up. Most members complete 3-4 bootcamps during their first year.
Who runs the bootcamps?
Ryan Stewart leads most bootcamps, with guest experts brought in for specialized topics like AI agent deployment. Every instructor has built and scaled agencies themselves -- no theorists or academics. You are learning from people who have done the exact thing you are trying to do.
