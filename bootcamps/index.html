Frequently Asked Questions

How often do bootcamps run?

New bootcamps launch monthly. Some are recurring series, others are one-time deep dives into specific topics. The schedule is posted in the member dashboard.

Are bootcamps recorded?

Yes. Every bootcamp is recorded and added to your training library within 48 hours. You get the full recording plus any templates or resources shared during the session.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Each bootcamp comes with a prep checklist sent before the session. Most just need you to show up with your laptop and your current agency setup.

Can I suggest a bootcamp topic?

Absolutely. Blueprint members vote on upcoming bootcamp topics. The most requested topics get scheduled first.
