Content is a huge part of SEO.

But you already knew that - everyone knows that.

Content used to be an effective strategy to increase traffic and revenue, but now the SERPs are saturated.

Now, AI has made things even worse...

  • Google's AI answers are stealing traffic that used to go to your website
  • AI writing tools are commoditizing the web, filling it with garbage
  • ​Clients are pushing back on content pricing, wanting it cheaper and faster

But...we still need content.

We just need the right content - better content.

As agencies, we need to build content that impacts conversions, not just rankings and traffic.

Old approach...

New approach...
