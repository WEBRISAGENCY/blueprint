    PART 2 · THE BUSINESS

    So...how do we adapt and win?

    • We fully embrace AI and we build now for the future
    • Human (you): strategy, sales, client relationships and quality
    • AI (employees): research, audit, writing, task execution, emailing, reporting
    • Subcontractors: specialist execution like paid management (short term until you build it into an agent)
    • Fewer salaries, higher margins, fewer clients served better. You can compete with the largest agencies. 

    The 5-year roadmap

    • Up to now: SEO only, sold on rankings and traffic
    • Now: blended search, own the whole results page
    • Year 2: full service, one retainer for all of the client's marketing
    • Year 3+: fractional CMO, you own the plan and the budget
    • This bootcamp gets you to blended search

    PART 3 · THE VERTICAL

    You have to select a niche...full stop

    • AI is only as good as its context. One niche means one set of prompts, skills, templates and data that get sharper with every client
    • Systems need repetition. You can only productize and hand work to agents when every client runs the same process
    • Generalist SEO is a commodity. AI can do generic work for anyone, so the value is in knowing one industry cold
    • Agents pick specialists. When an AI builds the shortlist, it recommends the expert in that space, not the agency that does everything
    • Blended search is niche-specific. LSA categories, keyword tables, click prices and intake are different in every industry
    • Proof compounds. Every case study sells the next client in the same niche

    Niche criteria: your niche must check every box

    • It lags behind and will always hire outside help. They will never learn to do this themselves. That makes them a done-for-you buyer (more in Part 4)
    • High customer value. In home services, roofing, not pressure washing
    • People still search and convert. Someone looking for a personal injury lawyer calls the first result and signs on the phone
    • Local and competitive. LSAs, ads, the map pack and organic all show up, so there's a whole page to win
    • Money for marketing and ad spend. Margins that can pay a retainer plus a real ad budget
    • Lots of independent businesses. Plenty of owner-run firms to sell to, not a market owned by a few national brands
    • A clear, trackable result. Calls, booked jobs or signed cases you can report a cost per acquisition on

      PART 4 · THE OFFER

      How offers work: information vs. labor

      • Information and labor are the 2 main ingredients in every offer, this model will help you guide all future business decisions you make
      • Those seeking an info offer have more time than money, so you sell the ROI of their time
      • Those seeking a done-for-you offer have more money than time, so you sell the ROI of their investment
      • A helpful exercise to understand how you will deliver and price, keeping in mind the trade off for lower labor offers usually will result in a lower pricing option (but not always)
      • The optimal offer is high information, low labor. You get paid for what you know, not for hours worked. This is the power of where are are moving, allowing AI agents to replace the heavy labor of the traditional agency model

      • Results are the only thing that matters. Calls, booked jobs and signed clients, not rankings

      • So we focus on the parts of search that still produce customers:

      PART 5 · UNDERSTANDING AI

      Models, context, prompts and QC

      • Pick the right model for the job
      • Context is everything: the same prompt gets a far better answer with the client's data loaded
      • Context comes from files, connectors, skills and client documents
      • A good prompt: role, task, context, format, examples
      • Nothing goes to a client unchecked. AI drafts, you approve

      Agents, tools, MCP and skills

      • Agents take steps on their own, not just answer questions
      • MCP plugs Claude or ChatGPT into your tools: Agency OS, Google Drive, ad accounts
      • Skills are saved processes that run the same way on every client
