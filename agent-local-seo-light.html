What It Does

The Local SEO Agent automates the complete local search workflow: Google Business Profile management, citation audits, NAP consistency checking, review monitoring, and response drafting across all major directories. Everything a local SEO campaign needs, packaged into one agent that scales with your client base.

You feed it your client's business data and current GBP profile. How It Works

The Local SEO Agent follows a complete 5-stage local search workflow that covers every deliverable from GBP optimization through monthly reporting:

1

GBP Audit & Optimization

Analyzes the client's Google Business Profile for completeness, accuracy, and optimization opportunities. Generates recommendations for categories, attributes, descriptions, and photo strategy to improve visibility and rankings.

2

Citation Audit & Cleanup

Crawls major directories and data aggregators to find existing citations. Identifies NAP inconsistencies, duplicate listings, and missing directories — then generates correction instructions for each platform and aggregator.

3

Review Monitoring & Response

Monitors new reviews across Google, Yelp, and industry-specific directories. Drafts personalized responses for each review — maintaining the client's brand voice while addressing specific feedback, whether positive, neutral, or negative.

4

Review Generation Strategy

Builds a review request workflow: identifies happy customers, generates request templates, and suggests optimal timing for outreach. Tracks review velocity and sentiment over time to show client impact and identify service improvements.

5

Monthly Local Reporting

Generates a local SEO performance report covering GBP insights, ranking positions for local keywords, review metrics, and citation health — formatted and ready for client delivery with actionable recommendations.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it manage GBP directly?

It generates all the optimization recommendations and content, but you apply the changes through the GBP dashboard. This keeps you in control of the client's listing.

How many local clients can I manage?

Members typically manage 15-25 local clients with this agent. The bottleneck shifts from manual work to strategic oversight.

Does it work for multi-location businesses?

Yes. The agent handles multi-location audits and generates location-specific recommendations. The training covers the multi-location workflow in detail.

Can it draft responses in the client's voice?

Yes. You train the agent on the client's brand voice and response style during setup. It adapts tone based on whether the review is positive, neutral, or negative.
