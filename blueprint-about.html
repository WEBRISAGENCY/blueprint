We flipped the agency model.

The Blueprint Training exists for one reason — to help SEO agency owners build AI-powered businesses that outperform, outdeliver, and outscale every traditional agency in their market.

The Old Model
  • Manual workflows that eat your time — keyword research, content briefs, audits, reporting all done by hand
  • Generalist teams with no deep expertise, slow output, and inconsistent results
  • Competing on price because your process looks the same as everyone else's
  • Hiring headcount to scale instead of building systems that do the work
The Blueprint Model
  • Purpose-built Claude agents that automate every workflow — from onboarding to link building to monthly reporting
  • Deep subject matter expertise baked into your agents so every deliverable reflects elite-level strategy
  • A fully differentiated service that's impossible to replicate without the same AI infrastructure
  • Scale by adding agents, not headcount — more clients, higher margins, same team

A system to deliver agency services that gets better results with less effort. It helps agencies simplify their business model by focusing on client impact, then building systems to productize that service.

$249/month or $2,499/year. Based on average member results, this is one of the highest-ROI investments an agency owner can make. We help agencies scale to $100K+ per month.

What's included in the program?

Video training, done-for-you templates, Claude AI agents, weekly live coaching with Ryan and David, private Pruvae community, monthly bootcamps, and direct Slack access to every coach.

You'll get more from the program with basic SEO knowledge, but you don't need to be an expert — we'll make you one. The program is built for those who want to offer SEO as a service and build proper systems around it.
