What It Does

Full outreach pipeline automation. The Link Building Agent handles prospect discovery, contact finding, personalized email drafting, and placement tracking. It maintains a pipeline view from prospected through outreach sent, response received, and live link secured. No more spreadsheets of prospects you never got around to emailing.

Why this matters

Link building is the most labor-intensive part of SEO. Most agencies either skip it entirely or do it poorly because they can't justify the hours. This agent makes it economically viable to run real outreach at scale — even as a solo operator.

How It Works

The Link Building Agent runs a five-step outreach pipeline, fully automated from prospect discovery through placement tracking:

1

Prospect Discovery

Based on the client's niche, target keywords, and competitor backlink profiles, the agent identifies high-quality link prospects: relevant blogs, resource pages, industry directories, and partnership opportunities.

2

Contact Finding

For each prospect, the agent identifies the right contact person and gathers outreach information — name, role, email, and any relevant context for personalization.

3

Personalized Outreach

Drafts customized outreach emails for each prospect. Not templates with [FIRST NAME] swapped in — genuinely personalized messages that reference the prospect's content and explain why the link makes sense.

4

Follow-Up Sequences

Generates follow-up emails at configured intervals for non-responders. Each follow-up adds new context or a different angle rather than just "bumping" the original email.

5

Pipeline & Placement Tracking

Tracks every prospect through the pipeline: prospected → outreach sent → responded → negotiating → link secured → live. Generates weekly pipeline reports so you know exactly where things stand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this actually send emails?

No. The agent drafts the emails and organizes your pipeline. You review and send from your own email client. This keeps you in control of the relationship and ensures deliverability.

How many prospects can it find per month?

Depends on the niche, but most members generate 50-100 qualified prospects per client per month. The quality filter is strict — it prioritizes relevance and authority over volume.

Can I customize the outreach templates?

Yes. The agent learns your preferred tone, angle, and offer structure. The training walks you through how to calibrate it for your specific approach to outreach.

Does it work for local link building?

Yes. The agent has specific modes for local prospecting — chambers of commerce, local directories, community organizations, and local media. It works alongside the Local SEO Agent.
