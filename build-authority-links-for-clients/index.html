Link building remains one of the most important ranking factors in Google, yet it's also one of the most complex aspect of SEO client services.

Our link acquisition training will teach you how to build authority links for your agency's clients, without hiring a huge team or expensive vendors.

→ GET THE TRAINING

Link building has always been hard for agencies

Link building presents a challenge for agencies, because:
  • Links building is time-consuming - Getting white hat (outreach) links requires a ton of resources, you need a team of people to find, vet and pitch the websites. If mismanaged, this can cost you a ton of money, time and headaches.
  • Link building is expensive - if you use link vendors to get links for you, it erodes your margins quickly. Most clients don't understand the value of link building so it's hard to make a profit reselling them.
  • Link building is difficult - it’s difficult finding “good” links on high-quality, relevant websites for one website. It's 100x more difficult doing that for dozens of clients.
Link acquisition is a problem you need to solve - your clients cannot rank in Google without them.

Is it possible to acquire links for SEO clients, profitably?

Yes, it is.

Last month, our agency WEBRIS acquired 267 links for our clients, without spending a dime on link vendors. We do not have a big, expensive outreach team - we have a single person (who is offshore and part-time).

There is a way to get great links for your clients without a team, vendors or mass outreach campaigns.

What you need is a database of links you can call on as needed.
Your own personal stash of relevant websites you can leverage to get links with a few clicks.
