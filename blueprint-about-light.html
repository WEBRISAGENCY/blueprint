2017 - 2019
The 5 Phases of SEO

Our original framework broke SEO delivery into five clear phases: technical, local, content, links, and reporting. It gave agencies a structured approach to client work for the first time, replacing guesswork with a repeatable process. Thousands of agencies adopted this as their foundation.

2020 - 2025
SEO Sprints

We took the 5 Phases and rebuilt them into a leaner, faster model designed for profitability. SEO Sprints turned delivery into a productized service with fixed timelines, standardized deliverables, and predictable margins. It became the industry standard for how modern SEO agencies operate.

Not at all. If you can follow a recipe, you can follow these modules. Every agent is pre-built and we walk you through deployment step-by-step. No coding required.

Yes, you'll need a Claude Pro or Team plan ($20-$25/month). This is separate from enrollment. We walk you through exactly which plan and tools to set up in Module 1.
Is this different from the main Blueprint program?
No, this is the same Blueprint Training you know, just updated. We've always kept the program current, and all previous material is still included in your purchase. The AI agent modules are the latest addition, built on top of the same proven SOPs and frameworks.

Most members have their first agent running within the first hour of Module 1. The onboarding agent is the easiest to start with, and the payoff is immediately visible.

That's exactly why we built lifetime updates into this program. As Claude evolves, the agents and training evolve with it. You pay once and stay current forever. We've been doing this for 9 years and we update constantly.

Yes. 30 days, no questions asked. If you go through the first three modules and don't believe this is going to change how you run your agency, email us and we'll refund you in full.
Can this work for a brand new agency?
Absolutely. Many Blueprint members have used our SOPs to launch their agencies from scratch. The AI layer makes it even more accessible, so you can deliver like a team of five on day one.
Does this work for niches outside SEO?
