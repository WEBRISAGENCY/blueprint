Common Questions
FREQUENTLY ASKED
What happens on the weekly coaching calls?
Each call is a mix of live Q&A, hot seats, and strategy sessions. You can bring specific challenges you are facing that week -- pricing a deal, handling a difficult client, deciding whether to hire -- and get real-time feedback from Ryan and the group. Calls are recorded so you can rewatch anything you miss.
How is this different from the video training?
The video training teaches you the system -- positioning, offers, sales, delivery. Coaching is where you apply it to your specific situation with hands-on guidance. Think of training as the playbook and coaching as having a coach on the sideline calling the plays with you in real time.
What if I can not make the live calls?
Every call is recorded and available within 24 hours. You can also submit questions in advance via Slack, and Ryan will answer them on the call even if you are not there. That said, the members who show up live consistently get the best results.
How much does coaching cost?
Coaching is included in your Blueprint Training membership at $199 per month. There are no upsells, no hidden tiers, and no long-term contracts. You get access to coaching, training, community, AI agents, and everything else in the program for one flat price.
What size agency is this best for?
Most coaching members are doing between $10K and $100K per month. Some are solo operators looking to build a small team, others have 5-15 employees looking to scale. The coaching adapts to where you are because the growth plan is personalized to your stage.
Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. There are no contracts or commitments. You can cancel your membership at any time directly from your account. Most members stay for 12+ months because the coaching continues to deliver value as their agency grows and faces new challenges at each stage.
