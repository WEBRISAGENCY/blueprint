The discovery call question list

What we ask, in order, on every first call with a prospect. The goal isn't to sell. It's to find out what they want, and whether you want to build them a proposal.

On the booking form
  • Are you actively investing in marketing?
  • If you work with an agency, how much time is left on your contract?
  • How much do you invest in marketing each month?
  • If we show you the roadmap, will you invest at least [your minimum] a month?
  • How did you hear about us?
15 minutes before
  • Review their booking-form answers and note your first thoughts.
  • Look at the website: team size, locations, services.
  • Drop the site into Ahrefs or Semrush, and a crawler if there's time.
  • Have one to three recommendations ready.
  • Start the recording.
Open
  • A few minutes of rapport: where are you based, how long have you been there?
  • "Tell me about the business and what you've done for marketing to date."
Dig in (let them talk)
  • What's worked? What didn't?
  • How did you measure that success?
  • Are you getting reports from your agency?
  • What do you want this to do for the business?
  • What's a new customer worth to you?
  • Who answers the calls and follows up?
  • How did you hear about us? Ask again, even if the form told you.
Close the call
  • Take two minutes to tell them who you are and your experience in their industry.
  • "Based on everything you told me, we're a great fit to work together."
  • Explain the next step: a deep analysis of their performance and competitors, reviewed together on a call.
  • Give them a price range so the proposal is no surprise.
  • Book the proposal call before you hang up.
The rule: the less you talk on the first call, the better it goes. This is their time to tell you what they want, what they expect and what went wrong before.
