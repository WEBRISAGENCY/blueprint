Frequently Asked Questions

How much does coaching cost?
The Inner Circle coaching program is $999/month. This includes everything in The Blueprint Training plus 1:1 onboarding, a custom growth track, regular coaching calls with Blueprint coaches, and direct access to Ryan's Inner Circle channel.
What is this call?
This is a 30-minute diagnostic call with Ryan Stewart. We'll talk about where your agency is today, where you want it to be, and whether the Blueprint coaching program is the right fit to get you there. There's no obligation — it's a conversation, not a sales pitch.
How does the coaching work?
Once accepted, you'll get a 90-minute deep dive onboarding session where we build a custom growth track for your agency. From there, you'll have regular 1:1 coaching calls, access to Ryan's Inner Circle Slack channel, and priority support on everything from positioning and pricing to delivery workflows and AI agent deployment.
What can I expect on this call?
We'll spend 30 minutes going through your current agency setup — services, team size, revenue, biggest bottlenecks. Then we'll map out what a coaching engagement would look like for you specifically: which areas to focus on, what AI agents to deploy first, and what a realistic growth timeline looks like. You'll leave with clarity either way.
