Frequently Asked Questions

What platform is the community on?

We use Slack for the community and Skool for course content. You'll get access to both when you join Blueprint.

How active is the community?

Very. There are conversations happening daily across channels. This isn't a ghost town — it's an active network of agency owners sharing wins, asking questions, and helping each other.

Can I hire from the community?

Yes. There's a dedicated jobs and contractors channel where members post opportunities and vetted freelancers respond. It's one of the most popular channels.

Is the community moderated?

Yes. Blueprint coaches and moderators keep conversations productive and on-topic. Zero tolerance for spam, negativity, or self-promotion.
