FAQ

Questions answered.

When was this program last updated?

Today. This is a living, breathing program. We are constantly updating trainings, SOPs, templates, and AI agents based on what's working right now. Nothing here is stagnant or out of date. When the industry shifts, we shift with it.

I'm a beginner. Is this too advanced?

Not at all. Many Blueprint members started from zero. The program walks you through positioning, offer design, how to land your first clients, and how to deliver results. You'll skip years of trial and error by following a system that's already been proven by thousands of agencies.

I have an agency, will this still help?

This is where it gets really powerful. If you're already doing the work, Blueprint gives you the systems to stop doing it all yourself. SOPs to delegate, templates to standardize, a hiring framework to build your team, and AI agents to automate the repetitive stuff. Most established members say they wish they had this years ago.

How is this different from other agency programs?

Most programs teach theory. Blueprint gives you the actual tools. You get the same SOPs, templates, and processes Ryan uses to run his own 8-figure agency. On top of that, the AI agents automate real work, not just concepts. 9 years and 3,000+ agencies later, the results speak for themselves.

How do I get clients?

The program covers both inbound and outbound in detail. You'll learn how to generate leads through content and low-spend paid ads on social, how to run a sales process that converts, and how to build proposals that close. Members regularly share wins from landing their first (or biggest) client using these exact methods.

What does the community look like?

It's a private group of agency owners who are in the trenches doing the same work you are. People share wins, ask questions, give feedback on proposals, and help each other solve real problems. It's not a hype group. It's a working group. You also get 2x weekly live coaching calls where you can get direct answers from the team.

Do I need to be technical to use the AI agents?

No. Every agent is pre-built and ready to go. We walk you through setup step-by-step. If you can follow instructions and click a few buttons, you can deploy your first agent. No coding required.

Do I need a Claude subscription?

Yes, you'll need a Claude Pro or Team plan ($20-$25/month). This is separate from your Blueprint membership. We walk you through exactly which plan to choose and how to set it up in Module 1.

What if I want to do more than SEO?

The core systems (positioning, sales, hiring, operations) apply to any service-based agency. The fulfillment training and AI agents are built specifically around SEO and local SEO, which is where Blueprint has the deepest track record. If that's your primary service, this is built for you.

Is there a money-back guarantee?

Yes. 30 days, no questions asked. If you go through the material and don't believe this is going to change how you run your agency, email us and we'll refund you in full.
