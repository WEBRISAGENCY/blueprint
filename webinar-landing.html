What We're Covering

60 minutes that could
change how you run
your agency.

The agency model is broken. You're doing too much, charging too little, and scaling the wrong way — by hiring people instead of building systems. In this live session, Ryan Stewart and David Krevitt are going to show you exactly how to flip that. How to use AI agents to automate 90% of the busy work — the audits, the content briefs, the reporting, the outreach, the project management — so you can focus on the 10% that actually grows the business.

You'll walk away knowing how to build and manage your own Claude agents that handle client work end to end, without a team of five behind you. This is the infrastructure that's allowing agency owners right now to run lean, deliver more, and scale past seven figures — with little to no overhead.

If you've been wondering how to actually use AI in your agency in a way that moves the needle — not just ChatGPT prompts, but real automated workflows built on your own SOPs — this is the session you've been waiting for.
