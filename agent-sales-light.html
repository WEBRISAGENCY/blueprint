What It Does

The Proposal Agent handles the most time-consuming part of your sales process: turning discovery call notes into a polished, branded proposal that's ready to present. No more spending three hours after every sales call building a custom deck from scratch.

You feed it your discovery notes and prospect data. It pulls competitive intelligence, builds a preliminary website quality audit, drafts a scope of work tailored to the prospect's needs, and packages everything into a branded proposal deck — formatted and ready for the client meeting.

Blueprint members using the Proposal Agent are closing deals faster because they're getting proposals out within hours of the discovery call, not days. When a prospect is still hot, that speed advantage is the difference between winning and losing the deal.

Why this matters

The longer it takes to send a proposal, the more likely the prospect is to go with someone else. Most agencies take 3-5 days. With this agent, you send it the same day — while the conversation is still fresh and the prospect's urgency is highest.

How It Works

The Proposal Agent follows the same 4-stage sales framework we've used to close millions in agency revenue. Here's what happens when you trigger it after a discovery call:

1

Discovery Notes Intake

You paste in your discovery call notes — prospect name, website, pain points, budget signals, timeline, and the 11 qualifying questions from the Blueprint sales framework. The agent structures everything it needs to build the proposal.

2

Competitive & Site Analysis

The agent pulls competitive data and runs a preliminary website quality assessment on the prospect's site — identifying quick wins, technical issues, and content gaps. This becomes the "proof" section of your proposal.

3

Scope & Pricing Generation

Based on the discovery data and site analysis, the agent drafts a recommended scope of work with value-based pricing — aligned to the prospect's stated goals. It uses Blueprint's pricing framework so you're quoting confidently, not guessing.

4

Branded Proposal Assembly

Everything is packaged into a branded proposal deck: executive summary, site analysis findings, recommended strategy, scope of work, pricing, timeline, and next steps. Ready to send or present on a follow-up call.

5

Onboarding Agent Handoff

When the prospect signs, the proposal data flows directly into the Onboarding Agent — client info, service scope, and contract terms are already structured. No re-entry, no gaps, no lost context between sale and delivery.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code to use this agent?

No. You paste your discovery notes into Claude's desktop interface, the agent asks a few follow-up questions if needed, and it generates the full proposal. No coding, no APIs, no technical setup.

Can I customize the proposal template for my brand?

Yes. The agent is trained on a default Blueprint proposal structure, but you can customize the formatting, sections, tone, and branding to match your agency. The training walks you through exactly how to make it yours.

How accurate is the competitive analysis?

The agent pulls publicly available data to build a preliminary competitive landscape — enough to demonstrate expertise and identify obvious opportunities in the prospect's market. It's not a full audit, but it's more than most agencies include in a proposal.

How long does it take to generate a proposal?

Most members go from pasting discovery notes to a finished proposal in under 20 minutes. Compare that to the 3-5 hours most agency owners spend building proposals manually — and the deals you lose in the meantime.

Does it work for services other than SEO?

The framework is built around SEO agency sales, but the discovery questions, pricing methodology, and proposal structure work for any digital service. Members have adapted it for PPC, content marketing, and web development proposals.
