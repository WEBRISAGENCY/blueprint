Common Questions
FREQUENTLY ASKED
How many video lessons are in the library?
There are currently over 300 video lessons organized across 10+ modules. New content is added every month, so the library is always growing. Each lesson is focused and actionable -- typically 10 to 20 minutes long.
Is the training self-paced or do I have to follow a schedule?
Completely self-paced. You can watch any module in any order, pause and come back whenever you want, and rewatch lessons as many times as you need. The learning paths give you a recommended order, but you are free to jump to whatever topic you need most right now.
Are the case studies from real agencies?
Yes. The case studies come from Ryan Stewart's own agency, WEBRIS, and from Blueprint Training members. You will see real screenshots, real numbers, and real strategies that were used on actual client campaigns -- not hypothetical examples.
How often is new content added?
New lessons are added every month. When Google rolls out an algorithm update, when a new tool changes the game, or when a member asks about a topic we have not covered yet -- we create new training to keep the library current and relevant.
What if I am a complete beginner?
The training library has a dedicated beginner learning path that starts with the fundamentals and builds from there. You do not need any prior agency experience. That said, the training is designed for people who want to run an agency -- if you are looking for generic SEO tutorials, this is not the right fit.
How much does access to the training library cost?
The training library is included in your Blueprint Training membership at $199 per month. That also gets you access to coaching, community, AI agents, live bootcamps, and everything else in the program. There are no upsells or separate tiers.
