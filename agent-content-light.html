Content is the most time-consuming deliverable in SEO. Most agencies either produce too little (because it takes too long) or produce too much low-quality content (because they're rushing). This agent gives you volume AND quality.

How It Works

The Content Agent follows a five-stage workflow to deliver strategic, SEO-optimized content at scale. Here's how it transforms content production from a bottleneck into a repeatable, high-quality system:

1

Content Audit & Gap Analysis

Analyzes the client's existing content against competitor coverage and keyword opportunities. Identifies gaps, thin content to improve, and pages that should be consolidated or removed.

2

Content Roadmap Generation

Builds a prioritized content calendar with topics mapped to target keywords, search intent, funnel stage, and estimated traffic impact. Every piece has a strategic reason for existing.

3

Outline & Brief Creation

For each content piece, generates a detailed outline with target keyword, secondary keywords, search intent, recommended word count, heading structure, and internal linking targets.

4

Draft Production

Writes full drafts based on the outlines — incorporating client context, industry expertise, and EEAT signals. Not generic AI content, but strategically structured pieces that match the client's voice.

5

On-Page Optimization

Applies technical on-page SEO: title tags, meta descriptions, schema markup recommendations, internal linking, image alt text, and readability optimization.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this produce generic AI content?

No. The agent is trained on your client's brand voice, industry context, and target audience. The training shows you exactly how to customize the output so it doesn't read like generic AI text.

How many pieces can it produce per month?

Most members produce 8-15 fully optimized pieces per client per month. The bottleneck shifts from production to review — which is where your expertise adds the most value.

Can I use it for different content types?

Yes. Blog posts, landing pages, service pages, location pages, and pillar content. The agent adapts the structure and approach based on the content type and search intent.

Does it handle images and media?

It generates image recommendations, alt text, and placement suggestions. You'll still need to source or create the actual images, but the agent tells you exactly what you need and where.
