What Is the Blueprint Video Training?

Most agency training teaches you what to do. The Blueprint teaches you how to do it — with process-focused video walkthroughs for every deliverable your agency needs to produce.

Over the past nine years, we've built a training platform that covers the complete lifecycle of running an SEO agency: finding your niche, productizing your service, delivering results at scale, acquiring clients, and closing deals. Every module is built around a real workflow, not a theory.

In 2024, we rebuilt the entire training library around AI. Every pillar now includes dedicated modules on how to use Claude AI agents to automate, accelerate, and improve each workflow — so you can do more with a smaller team, get results faster, and deliver a service your competitors can't replicate.

Who this is for

SEO agencies, consultants, and freelancers who want to learn how to integrate AI into their service delivery, build systems that run without them, and position their agency to charge premium prices in a market that's rapidly changing.

Why AI Training for Agencies — and Why Now

The SEO agency model is being rebuilt. Workflows that once required a team of five — keyword research, content briefs, technical audits, link prospecting, monthly reporting — can now be handled by a single operator using AI agents trained on your exact SOPs.

The agencies winning right now aren't the ones with the biggest teams. They're the ones who've figured out how to layer AI on top of proven processes. That's exactly what this training covers.

We don't teach you to use generic ChatGPT prompts. We teach you to build purpose-built Claude agents that know your service, your clients, and your delivery standards — and run your workflows without you having to supervise every step.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to know how to code to use the AI training?

No. Every AI module is built for operators, not developers. We use Claude's Cowork and Desktop interfaces — no coding required. If you can follow a recipe, you can deploy these agents.

Do I need a Claude subscription separately?

Yes. You'll need a Claude Pro or Team plan (~$20–$25/month) in addition to your Blueprint membership. We walk you through exactly which plan to get and how to set it up in the first module.

Is SEO Sprints included?

Yes. The complete SEO Sprints training is included under Pillar 3 — rebuilt with AI automation modules for every deliverable. It's the most comprehensive module in the library.

How is the AI content different from generic ChatGPT training?

We don't teach you to use AI as a writing assistant. We teach you to build agents trained on your specific SOPs, client context, and delivery standards — so they produce agency-quality output, not generic AI text.
