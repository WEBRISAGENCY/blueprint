The One Person Sales Team Process

This is the exact 4-stage system I use to close $5K-$15K/month SEO deals. Each stage has specific training, templates, and live examples so you can implement it immediately.

01
Stage 1

Mastering The Discovery Call

Before you invest a single hour, you need to know if this prospect is worth your time. The discovery call is a 30-minute qualification conversation -- not a pitch. You'll learn the exact questions to ask, how to estimate pricing from 5 minutes on their homepage plus 2 minutes in Ahrefs, and the gate that ensures you never build a proposal for someone who hasn't already agreed to your ballpark.

What's included
2 training videos Discovery call script Qualification checklist Pricing estimation template
02
Stage 2

Analysis & Proposal Production

This is where most agencies waste the most time. You'll learn how to build a 30-slide, data-driven analysis deck that does the selling for you -- competitive gaps, keyword opportunities, backlink profiles, local rankings, and a 12-month traffic projection. The AI proposal agent handles the heavy lifting so you (or a team member) can produce this in under an hour instead of three.

What's included
3 training videos AI Proposal Agent 30-slide proposal template Traffic projection calculator
03
Stage 3

Proposal Review + Close

Walk the prospect through your findings live and let the data make the case. Because you already discussed pricing on the discovery call, there's no sticker shock. You'll learn the assumptive close -- "when would be a good start date?" -- how to present month-to-month terms as confidence (not weakness), and how to handle the most common objections without sounding salesy.

What's included
2 training videos Recorded sales calls Objection handling scripts Pricing breakdown template
04
Stage 4

Follow-Up + Deal Management

Most deals don't close on the first call -- they close in the follow-up. You'll learn the system that keeps deals from going dark, why you've earned the right to follow up aggressively after doing the work upfront, and how to run no-contract, month-to-month engagements with near-zero churn. If you don't deserve to keep the client, you don't deserve the contract.

1 training video Follow-up email sequences Deal tracking framework Q&A recordings
