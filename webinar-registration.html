“AI can be a trap. You work a lot faster, but you also end up working a lot more.

The key to leveraging it is having the right process on the input side — the ability to templatize and productize, then share that across everyone who makes an agency go: full-time employees, contractors, freelancers.

Everyone who comes into your business needs to be able to maintain the quality. Otherwise you just end up creating slop.

Webris is all about how to use AI effectively to improve your service.”
