Built for the needs of agencies

Product tools assume one team shipping one roadmap. An agency runs fifteen client plans at once, each with its own scope, cadence, and reporting — so the tool never fits, and the real plan ends up in a spreadsheet nobody updates.

Agency OS is shaped like retainer work: every client gets a project, projects hold sprints, sprints hold tasks, and tasks hold the subtasks and checklists that make "done" unambiguous.

01 · The structure
Deliver client work the way your agency sells it

Every client gets a project with a status and a primary goal. Inside it, sprints hold the tasks, and each client carries its own tabs — Tasks, Content, Links, Analyses, and Data. Every tab is deep-linkable, so a Slack message can open the exact view you're talking about.

03 · Subtasks & recurring work
The work that repeats sets itself up

Subtasks expand under the parent row with their own owner, date, and status, and each one can hold a nested checklist. Finish the last subtask and the parent completes itself — reopen one and it reopens too. Set anything to recur daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly, and the next occurrence spawns the moment you close the current one.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you move your client work over.

Can I import from Asana, ClickUp, or Monday?

Asana import is live — projects, tasks, subtasks, owners, and dates come across with your structure intact. ClickUp and Monday plug into the same layer. You can also export any plan to ClickUp, Asana, Monday, or CSV, and free migration support is included with every membership.

Can I use my own project templates?

Yes. Six service plans ship ready to apply as a sprint — Onboarding, SEO, Web Design, Facebook Ads, PPC Ads, and LSA Ads — and you can build your own in Settings with a builder that mirrors the real tasks table. Approved findings from a website or local SEO audit can also schedule themselves into a 12-month plan.

Can I change the statuses and columns?

Yes. Rename, recolor, and reorder your statuses once and they apply across the Table, List, and Board views. Add your own text columns per project, resize them, and drag rows into the order you want.

How does recurring work work?

Set any task or subtask to recur daily, weekly, biweekly, or monthly. Completing an occurrence spawns the next one with its owner, dates, and subtasks attached. Parent tasks complete themselves when the last subtask is done, and reopen if one gets reopened.

Can clients see the plan?

Yes, on your terms. Share a read-only link that exposes only the sections you choose — Tasks, Content, Links, Performance — with an optional label and passcode, and revoke it whenever you want. Internal notes, estimates, and capacity stay on your side.

Can Claude create and update tasks?

Yes. Through the connector, Claude can list and update projects and sprints, create and update tasks with validated dependencies, apply plan templates, and read the content calendar. Ask for the pulse report and it returns overdue and blocked work, stale data, analyses awaiting review, and anyone over capacity. Tasks can be marked as run by Claude or by a human.

Is project management sold separately?

No. It's part of Agency OS, which is included in your $199/mo Blueprint Training membership along with the CRM, client reporting, billing, the AI agent suite, and the community.
