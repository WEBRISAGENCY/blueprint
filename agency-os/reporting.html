Built for the needs of agencies

Most reporting tools were built to visualize one channel. Agencies sell seven — SEO, local, links, content, PPC, LSA, and paid social — and end up stitching a report together out of four dashboards, two spreadsheets, and a screenshot the night before the call. Agency OS reports on every service you sell, in one place, per client. The data pulls itself in, the report carries your brand, and the client can open it any day of the month instead of waiting for it.

01 · The report
One report, every channel you sell

The report is sectioned by service — SEO, local, links, content, PPC, LSA, paid social — and each client only shows the sections you actually run for them. Pick a date range, hit refresh to pull live, and the whole thing rebuilds against the new period with the previous-period comparison already calculated.

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you move your client reporting over.

Where does the data come from?

Sources broker through Windsor.ai — GA4, Search Console, Google Business Profile, Google Ads, Local Services Ads, and Meta — with Ahrefs, Local Falcon, and DataForSEO for rankings, links, and grid tracking. You connect each source once, then paste the account IDs on the client's Data tab.

Which channels can I report on?

SEO (traffic, clicks, keywords, leads), local SEO (grid heat maps, GBP rankings, calls, clicks), links (placements and the performance of the pages they point at), content (what you published and what it earned), PPC, LSA, and paid social (clicks, conversions, cost per result, best-performing ads). Each client only shows the sections you run for them.

Is the report white-labelled?

Yes. Upload your logo and set your accent colour once and every client report carries your brand, not ours — as does the app itself.

Can clients see it themselves?

Yes. Each client report has a live share link, so they can check performance any day of the month instead of waiting for a monthly PDF. You control which sections they see.

What is Generate analysis?

One button reads the whole report and writes what's working, what isn't, and why — then saves it to the client's Analyses tab. It runs on your own Claude plan through the connector, so there's no separate API key and no separate bill.

How far back does the data go?

As far back as each source allows. Search Console history tops out around 15 months — Google's limit, not ours — so that's the longest preset for search data. Reports read from a cache, so hit Refresh to pull the latest.

Is reporting sold separately?

No. It's part of Agency OS, which is included in your $199/mo Blueprint Training membership along with the CRM, project management, billing, the AI agent suite, and the community.
