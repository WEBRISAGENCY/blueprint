Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know before you move your pipeline over.

Can I change the pipeline stages?

You can reorder columns and hide the ones you don't use, and the layout saves to your account. The nine stages themselves are fixed — that's deliberate. Automation, follow-up logic, and revenue reporting all key off them, so keeping them consistent is what makes the numbers trustworthy.

Can I import my existing CRM?

Yes. Import your pipeline and map your old CRM's stage names onto the Agency OS stages during setup. Leads dedupe on email, so re-imports won't create duplicates. Free migration support is included with every membership.

How do new leads get into the board?

Three ways: add them by hand, import them in bulk, or let Claude sync them. A scheduled prompt reads your booking tool and creates or updates each lead with contact info, qualification answers, and the discovery-call time.

What happens when I win a deal?

Moving a deal to Won converts the lead into a live client automatically, carrying the notes, calls, and proposals across. Reopen and re-win it later and the client reactivates. You also get a full-screen celebration, which is the correct response.

Does it work outside the US?

Yes. Pick your currency in settings and deal values, pipeline totals, and revenue reporting all follow it.

Is the CRM sold separately?

No. It's part of Agency OS, which is included in your $199/mo Blueprint Training membership along with project management, client reporting, billing, the AI agent suite, and the community.
