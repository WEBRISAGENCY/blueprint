Questions?

What's actually included in the membership?
Everything you need to run and grow an agency in one place: Agency OS (the software your agency runs on), the full training suite covering sales, fulfillment and AI, the SOP and template library, the done-for-you AI agent suite, a private community of 3,000+ agency owners, and 2x weekly live coaching calls with Ryan Stewart and David Krevitt. One price, unlimited access — and every future update is included.
What is Agency OS, and what does it replace?
Agency OS is the software your agency runs on — clients, projects, reporting and billing in a single platform. It replaces the patchwork of CRM, project management, reporting and client-portal tools most agencies duct-tape together, for a fraction of the combined cost. It also connects directly to Claude, so you can hand off real work like onboarding, audits, proposals and reporting.
Do I need a Claude subscription?
Yes — a Claude Pro or Team plan ($20–$25/month), billed separately from Blueprint. It's what powers the AI agents and the Claude integration inside Agency OS. We show you exactly which plan to pick and how to set it up in Module 1.
Do I need to be technical to use the software and agents?
No. Agency OS and every AI agent are pre-built and ready to use — if you can follow a checklist and click a few buttons, you're set. And when you join, we handle free data migration to move your existing clients and projects into the platform for you.
I'm a beginner. Is this too advanced?
Not at all. Plenty of members started from zero. You'll get a clear path through positioning, offer design, landing your first clients and delivering results — skipping years of trial and error by following a system that's already been proven by thousands of agencies.
I already run an agency. Will this still help?
This is where it gets powerful. Blueprint gives you the systems to stop doing everything yourself: SOPs to delegate, templates to standardize, a hiring framework, AI agents to automate the repetitive work, and the software to run it all. Most established members say they wish they'd had it years ago.
How do I actually get clients?
The program covers inbound and outbound in detail — generating leads through content and low-spend paid social, running a sales process that converts, and building proposals that close. Members regularly share wins from landing their first (or biggest) client using these exact methods.
How is this different from other agency programs?
Most programs sell theory. Blueprint gives you the actual tools — the same SOPs, templates, software and processes Ryan uses to run his 8-figure agency, plus AI agents that do real work. 9 years and 3,000+ agencies later, the results speak for themselves.
What if my agency does more than SEO?
The core systems — positioning, sales, hiring, operations and the software itself — apply to any service-based agency. The fulfillment training and AI agents go deepest on SEO and local SEO, where Blueprint has the strongest track record. If that's your primary service, it's built for you.
Is there a contract? Can I cancel?
No contracts. It's month to month at $199 and you can cancel anytime. You keep full access to the software, training, agents, community and coaching for as long as you're a member — including all future updates, new agents and releases.
