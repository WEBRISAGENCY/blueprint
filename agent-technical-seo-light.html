What It Does

The most robust agent in the Blueprint library. It crawls a client's site, runs a full technical audit covering crawlability, indexation, site speed, schema, internal linking — then layers on content quality analysis, EEAT signal evaluation, and link profile assessment. The output is a prioritized findings report with specific recommendations, not a generic checklist.

You provide a client URL and the agent initiates a comprehensive crawl. It collects technical data across crawlability, indexation, site architecture, page speed, mobile usability, and schema markup. Then it evaluates that data against Blueprint's technical SEO standards, identifies issues, categorizes them by severity, and maps each finding to its impact on rankings and user experience.

But it goes beyond the technical layer. The agent analyzes content quality signals, thin content, duplicate content, and EEAT indicators across the site. It evaluates the backlink profile, internal linking structure, anchor text distribution, and identifies toxic or low-quality links. Everything gets packaged into a client-ready audit report with executive summary, prioritized findings by impact, specific recommendations, and a suggested implementation timeline.

How It Works

The WQA Agent follows a five-stage process. Here's what happens when you feed it a client URL:

1

Site Crawl & Data Collection

You provide the client URL and the agent initiates a comprehensive crawl. It collects technical data across crawlability, indexation, site architecture, page speed, mobile usability, and schema markup.

2

Technical Health Analysis

The agent evaluates crawl data against Blueprint's technical SEO standards. It identifies issues, categorizes them by severity, and maps each finding to its impact on rankings and user experience.

3

Content & EEAT Evaluation

Goes beyond technical — analyzes content quality signals, thin content, duplicate content, and EEAT indicators (authorship, citations, expertise signals) across the site.

4

Link Profile Assessment

Evaluates the site's backlink profile, internal linking structure, anchor text distribution, and identifies toxic or low-quality links that need attention.

5

Prioritized Report Generation

Packages everything into a client-ready audit report with executive summary, prioritized findings by impact, specific recommendations, and a suggested implementation timeline.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a full audit take?

Most members generate a complete audit in 1-2 hours. Compare that to the 8-15 hours most agencies spend doing it manually.

Can I white-label the audit report?

Yes. The output is fully customizable — add your branding, adjust the sections, change the formatting to match your agency's style.

Does it replace manual review?

It handles 80-90% of the work. You should still review the findings and add your expert commentary before presenting to the client — that's where your expertise adds the most value.

What tools does it integrate with?

The agent works with data from Screaming Frog, Ahrefs, Search Console, and PageSpeed Insights. The training covers exactly how to feed this data in.
