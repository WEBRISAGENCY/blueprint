Common Questions
FREQUENTLY ASKED
What format are the templates in?
Templates come in the format that makes sense for each document -- Google Docs, Google Sheets, Notion, and downloadable PDFs. You can duplicate them into your own workspace and customize them however you want. Every template includes a video walkthrough showing how to use it.
Can I customize these for my agency branding?
Absolutely. Every template is designed to be white-labeled. Swap in your logo, colors, and company name. The structure and content are the hard part -- that is already done for you. Most members have their branded versions ready within an hour.
How often are templates updated?
The library is updated quarterly as best practices evolve. When Google rolls out algorithm changes, when new tools emerge, or when we find a better way to do something, the templates get updated. You always have access to the latest versions.
Do I get access to all templates with my membership?
Yes. Every template, SOP, and worksheet in the library is included in your $199/month Blueprint Training membership. There are no add-ons, no premium tiers, and no extra charges. You also get access to coaching, video training, AI agents, and the community.
Are there video walkthroughs for each template?
Yes. Every template includes a video walkthrough where Ryan walks through the document, explains the thinking behind each section, and shows you how to customize it for your agency. You are never left guessing how to use something.
What if I need a template that is not in the library?
Request it. We actively take suggestions from members and add new templates based on what the community needs. Drop your request in Slack and if enough members need it, we build it. Many of the current templates started as member requests.
