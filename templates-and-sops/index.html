How it works

Find it. Apply it. Make it yours.

1

Find it

Search the Template Suite by category, or open the Library in Agency OS and pick the plan or generator.

2

Apply it

Plans apply to a client with a start date. Generators run from the client or lead data. Docs copy to your Drive.

3

Change a template in Settings → Project Plans or in your copy of the doc and every future use carries your version.

Questions answered.

Are these Google Docs or something else?
Docs, sheets, and scripts live in the Template Suite as Google files you copy. Plans, checklists, and generators live inside Agency OS as working features, so you apply them rather than copy them.
Can I customize the templates?
Yes. Copy and edit any doc. Edit any plan template in Settings → Project Plans and your version becomes the default for your agency.
How do I get my existing SOPs in?
Build a custom plan in Settings → Project Plans, or ask Claude to import a sheet or ClickUp export into a plan. Free migration help is included.
How often are templates updated?
Whenever WEBRIS changes the way we do the work. Updates are announced in the community.
Are templates sold separately?
No. They are part of the $199/mo membership with Agency OS, coaching, training, agents, and the community.
