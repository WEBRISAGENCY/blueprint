What It Does

The Reporting Agent connects to your clients' GA4, Search Console, Google Business Profile, call tracking, and rank tracking tools — then generates fully branded monthly reports with performance data AND written narratives that explain what happened, why it matters, and what's planned next.

Most agencies lose clients because clients don't see the work. It's not that the SEO is bad — it's that clients never get a clear picture of what was done and what it accomplished. This agent solves that by automating the entire reporting process: data pull, analysis, narrative writing, and scheduled delivery.

Set up a client's data connections once, configure the reporting schedule, and the agent handles everything else. Reports arrive in their inbox on whatever cadence they expect — weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly — without you thinking about it.

How It Works

The Reporting Agent follows a five-step process: connect data sources, analyze performance, write narratives, assemble the report, and deliver on schedule. Here's what happens each reporting cycle:

1

Data Connection & Pull

Connects to the client's GA4, Search Console, GBP, call tracking, and rank tracking tools. Pulls all performance data for the reporting period automatically.

2

Metric Analysis

Analyzes the data for trends, anomalies, and significant changes. Identifies what improved, what declined, and what needs attention — with context on why.

3

Narrative Generation

Writes human-readable performance narratives for each section. Not just "traffic went up 12%" — but explaining what drove the increase, which pages contributed, and how it connects to the work delivered.

4

Report Assembly

Packages everything into a branded report template: executive summary, traffic overview, keyword rankings, content performance, conversion data, and next month's priorities.

5

Scheduled Delivery

Reports can be generated on any cadence — weekly, bi-weekly, or monthly. Set it once per client and the agent delivers on schedule without you thinking about it.

Frequently Asked Questions

What tools does it connect to?

GA4, Google Search Console, Google Business Profile, and most major rank tracking tools. The training walks you through connecting each data source.

Can I customize the report template?

Yes. You control the sections, branding, and level of detail. Some members create different templates for different client tiers.

Does it write the narrative sections automatically?

Yes. The agent generates performance narratives that explain what happened and why. You can review and edit before sending, or send as-is once you trust the output.

How long does it take to generate a report?

Under 10 minutes per client once data sources are connected. Compare that to the 2-3 hours most agencies spend per report manually.
