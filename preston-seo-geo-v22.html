What Is an AI Citation?
When someone asks ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI, or Gemini a question, the AI generates an answer by pulling from websites it considers authoritative. Those websites are "cited." A citation means your content appeared in an AI answer — reaching prospects who never click a blue link. More citations = more clients reached through AI search.
