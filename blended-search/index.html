Is SEO Dead? Why Your Agency Needs To Sell Blended Search

Ryan Stewart
Founder, The Blueprint Training
Published: Nov 6, 2026
Read: 10 min

Almost 70% of Google searches now end without a click, according to Similarweb data reported by Digiday.

If you run an SEO agency and that stat doesn't make the hair on your neck stand up, you have a problem.

You can't sell SEO like it's 2012 anymore. Optimize the site, write some content, build some links, send the report. Clients won't keep paying for that.

I've run an agency for almost 20 years. I'm not making another "SEO is dead" video for clicks. But I'll be honest: we're close.

Here's the short version:

  • Google is becoming a visibility platform, not a click platform.
  • Your clients' websites now sit under ads, a Maps pack and directories like Yelp and Angi.
  • People still need the services your clients sell. They just find them in different places.
  • The fix is blended search: PPC, LSA, local SEO, traditional SEO and AI search, sold as one service.
  • Paid data lets you forecast outcomes, and paid leads buy you time while SEO builds.

Is SEO Dead?

SEO as a standalone service is dying. Search isn't. Google still gets the searches, but fewer of them end with a click to your client's website.

Similarweb found that zero-click news searches on Google rose from 56% in May 2024 to nearly 69% by May 2025, the year after AI Overviews launched in the U.S.

The number of searches might be the same. The number of outcomes is falling through the floor.

So the question isn't whether SEO works. It's whether SEO alone can keep a client paying you.

Zero-Click Search Is the New Normal

A zero-click search is a search that ends on Google. The person gets what they need from the results page and leaves.

Pew Research Center tracked the real browsing of 900 U.S. adults. When an AI summary showed up, people clicked a regular result 8% of the time, versus 15% without one.

Read that again. The click your SEO work used to earn is getting cut in half.

Where Your Client's Website Actually Ranks

Look at the organic results. Yelp. The Home Depot. A national franchise. The Better Business Bureau. Angi.

The actual local HVAC company's website was 8th in the organic results. Under all of the ads, the Maps pack and People Also Ask.

If you're still treating SEO the way you did five years ago, even two years ago, that's what you're selling: a spot at the bottom of page one.

The AI Search Gorilla in the Room

And that's just Google. We haven't even talked about AI search.

OpenAI reported more than 900 million weekly ChatGPT users in February 2026. I use ChatGPT for almost all of my own searches now.

Google is probably headed to a chat interface too. The traditional ranking results won't look like this forever.

Human Behavior Hasn't Changed. The Path to Hire Has.

People still need HVAC companies. They still need attorneys. They still buy white shoes online.

That part hasn't changed. What changed is how they get to the decision of who to hire.

It used to be simple: search Google, click, call. Now one search splits across half a dozen placements on Google, plus AI search, plus social.

My TikTok feed sends me content about anything I've talked about before I even think to search for it. Your clients' customers are living the same way.

Your Clients Won't Pay for Diminishing Returns

I get it. You've been doing SEO a long time. Nobody wants to learn new skills or rebuild their business model.

But clients won't keep paying to watch their results shrink. And they won't keep paying when they're overpromised and underdelivered nonstop.

Your job as an agency is simple. Make your clients money. That's why they pay you for SEO in the first place.

Two years ago, if you ranked first, maybe 6 out of 10 searchers landed on your client's site. Now it's closer to one. With zero-click rates climbing, it's going lower.

That's what hits your churn and your MRR. More on keeping clients longer in how to land bigger agency clients.

What Is the Blended Search Model?

The blended search model takes every opportunity search gives you and rolls it into a single service. PPC, LSA, local SEO, traditional SEO and AI search.

People are still using Google. You're just going after every placement on the page, not one.

This isn't an indictment of what we do. It's the evolution of what we do.

The Roulette Rule

Think about roulette. You don't walk into a casino, put every chip on red and decide you're about to be a millionaire.

You spread your chips across the board. That's exactly what blended search is.

Why Blended Search Is Easier to Sell

When you bring paid into the conversation, you can talk about outcomes.

Paid search data and LSA data, combined with SEO data, let you build a real forecast. "If you spend one, you'll make two."

That's a very powerful conversation. It helps you sell more. More importantly, it gets clients results. We break down the math in how to calculate SEO ROI.

Sell Outcomes, Not Visibility Metrics

If you sell SEO today, you're probably reporting visibility scores out of an SEO tool. That stuff doesn't pay the bills.

The estimates in those tools can be way off, and clients don't care about them anyway. They care about leads and customers.

Paid Buys You Time While SEO Builds

Combine the long-term power of organic with the short-term performance of PPC and LSA.

The client gets leads and phone calls within the first week. That gives you the time and breathing room to get the organic side flowing.

That's the difference between a client who churns in month four and one who stays for years.

How to Add PPC and LSA If You've Never Run Them

I know what you're thinking. "I don't do PPC. I've never run LSA. I don't have the people for it."

PPC and LSA are the easiest services to subcontract. In my experience you can pay someone $200 to $300 per account to set up and run the ads.

You keep the strategy and the client relationship. They run the campaigns.

How to Pitch Blended Search to Your Clients

It starts with you. The expert, the consultant, the mouthpiece of the agency.

Your job is to educate clients and prospects on exactly what's in this article. Not to bury your head in the sand.

Open their main keyword on the call. Walk them through every placement. Show them where they actually sit.

When I teach this, agencies get mad at me for calling it out. But Google doesn't care about you winning. I do. And that means being honest about how search works now.

The proposal that goes with this pitch is in our SEO proposal guide.

Why Agencies Aren't Going Anywhere

No matter what happens with AI, my clients are still going to need my team's help to make sense of this world.

They might use ChatGPT for some things. I can build a website talking to it on the treadmill. Most business owners still won't do it. They have too much going on.

So they'll rely on you to be the expert. But you can't pretend things are okay. You know the meme with the dog sitting in the burning room saying "this is fine"? It's not fine.
