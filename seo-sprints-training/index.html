Common Questions
FREQUENTLY ASKED
What exactly is an SEO Sprint?
An SEO Sprint is a focused 30-day engagement where you deliver one specific area of SEO -- like technical fixes, content creation, or link building -- with defined deliverables, a fixed price, and a clear timeline. It replaces the vague monthly retainer with something clients can actually understand and buy.
How much should I charge per sprint?
Most agencies price sprints between $5,000 and $15,000 depending on the sprint type, complexity, and their market positioning. The training includes a pricing calculator that helps you figure out the right price based on your costs, target margins, and competitive landscape.
Can I still keep my retainer clients?
Absolutely. Many agencies run sprints alongside existing retainers. In fact, the sprint model is a great way to upsell retainer clients on specific projects. Over time, most agencies find sprints more profitable and gradually transition their entire model.
Do I need a big team to run sprints?
No. Solo operators and small teams can run sprints just as effectively as larger agencies. The SOPs and checklists are designed to be followed by anyone with basic SEO knowledge. Start with one sprint type, master it, then expand as you grow.
What if a sprint takes longer than 30 days?
The sprint framework includes buffer time and scope management techniques to keep you on track. If a client needs more work than what fits in one sprint, you scope a second sprint -- which is exactly how the stacking model creates long-term revenue.
